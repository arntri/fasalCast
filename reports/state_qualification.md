# State Qualification Probe — Food Access Watch (Scope-Correction Task 3)

Probe year **2024**. Status: **COMPLETE**.

**Pre-registered rule (fixed before probing):** a state QUALIFIES for phase A if **>= 5 mandis** show dense reporting (**>= 150 price records** in the probe year) together covering **>= 5 of 8 crop groups**.

**Hard caps:** top 5 districts by cereal market count, top 3 mandis per district, one year, PRICES only, every /markets response cached.

## Dropped from phase A (structural, not appetite)

- **Bihar (state 10)** — APMC (regulated mandi) Act repealed 2006; structurally few reporting markets. Measured: 4 of 8 crop groups absent, e.g. wheat reported by only 6 mandis vs 286 in MP.
- **Karnataka (state 29)** — most trade flows through the state's own e-market platform (ReMS / Unified Market Platform), bypassing central Agmarknet. Measured: ~37-68 reporting days per mandi per year vs 150-290 in the kept states.
